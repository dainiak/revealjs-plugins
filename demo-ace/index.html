6. @! — context lit at slide load

7. @K+ — sticky from step K

DEBUG appears at step 1 and stays lit through every later step, co-highlighted with each new function as it's introduced. Without the + it would go off at step 2.

8. @K- — on at load, off after step K

records is lit at slide load and stays through each transformation; step 4 explicitly drops it and the highlight clears. The off-fade lands on the existing @4 anchor — no extra click.
